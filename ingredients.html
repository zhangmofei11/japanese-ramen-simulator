Ingredients Guide

Noodles

  • Thin Straight - For light broths, quick cooking time
  • Thick Wavy - Holds thick broth well, chewy texture
  • Curly (Miso style) - Traps sauce, popular in Hokkaido
  • Straight Thick - Traditional Kyushu style

Proteins

  • Pork Bones - For tonkotsu broth, simmer 12+ hours
  • Chicken Bones - Base for lighter broths
  • Chashu Pork - Braised pork belly, served sliced
  • Ground Pork - For miso ramen topping
  • Eggs - Soft-boiled for ajitsuke tamago

Vegetables & Garnishes

  • Green Onions - Sliced thin for topping
  • Corn - Common in miso ramen
  • Bamboo Shoots (Menma) - Fermented bamboo
  • Bean Sprouts - Adds crunch
  • Nori - Seaweed sheets
  • Spinach - Blanched for shio ramen

Suppliers

  • Butcher Shop - Pork bones, chashu, chicken
  • Fish Market - Dried sardines, kombu for dashi
  • Grocery Store - Noodles, vegetables, eggs
  • Specialty Shop - Authentic Japanese ingredients

Storage Tips

  • Fresh noodles - Use within 24 hours
  • Dried noodles - Store in cool, dry place
  • Pork bones - Refrigerate up to 3 days
  • Chashu - Keeps 5 days refrigerated
  • Vegetables - Most keep 1 week in fridge
