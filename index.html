ABOUT THE GAME

THE EXPERIENCE

Japanese Ramen Simulator brings the authentic Tokyo ramen shop experience to life. Run your own restaurant in a virtual Tokyo yokocho (bar alley), prepare authentic ramen from scratch, and serve customers from around the world.

KEY FEATURES

  • • Physics-based cooking mechanics
  • • Up to 6 player co-op multiplayer
  • • Full shop customization
  • • Menu beyond ramen: gyoza, rice bowls, beer
  • • Random events and challenges
  • • First and third-person views
