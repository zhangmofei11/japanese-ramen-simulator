Multiplayer Guide

Recommended Team Roles

  • Head Chef - Coordinates kitchen, manages orders
  • Noodle Cook - Boils noodles to perfect timing
  • Broth Master - Prepares and seasons broths
  • Topping Specialist - Prepares and plates toppings
  • Server - Takes orders, serves customers
  • Cashier - Handles payments, tips, and upgrades

Co-op Strategies

  • Assembly line - Each person handles one component
  • Communication - Call out orders and timing
  • Prep during downtime - Prepare toppings between orders
  • Backup system - Know how to cover other roles

Communication Tips

  • Use spatial voice chat for in-game communication
  • Call out "order up" when ramen is ready
  • Warn teammates about angry customers
  • Announce when running low on ingredients
  • Coordinate upgrades and purchases

Handling Rush Hours

  • Focus on speed over perfection
  • Skip complex recipes during rush
  • Server takes orders while kitchen cooks
  • Keep simple broth ready as backup
  • Don't let customers leave angry - prioritize them
