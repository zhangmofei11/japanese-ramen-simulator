COOKING TIPS & TRICKS

Master the art of ramen with these essential cooking techniques

🍜 Noodle Boiling Guide

Perfect Boiling Times

  • • Thin noodles (细面): 45-60 seconds
  • • Medium noodles (中粗面): 60-90 seconds
  • • Thick noodles (太面): 90-120 seconds
  • • Wavy noodles (波浪面): 75-100 seconds

Pro tip: Test a single noodle to check texture before draining

Water & Salt

  • • Use 1L water per 100g noodles
  • • Add 1% salt to boiling water
  • • Never add oil - it prevents sauce adhesion
  • • Stir once at 30 seconds to prevent sticking

🔥 Broth Mastery

Tonkotsu Base

Boil pork bones for 12+ hours at rolling boil. The milky white color comes from emulsified fat.

  • • 2kg pork bones
  • • High heat throughout
  • • Skim foam every hour

Shoyu Tare

Classic soy sauce seasoning base. Balance salty, sweet, and umami flavors.

  • • 200ml soy sauce
  • • 50ml mirin
  • • 20ml sake

Miso Ramen

Fermented soybean paste adds depth. Start with 2 tbsp per bowl and adjust.

  • • White miso for sweet
  • • Red miso for intense
  • • Mix for balance

🥢 Topping Techniques

Chashu Pork

Roll and tie pork belly tightly before braising. Sear until golden, then simmer in soy-mirin broth for 2 hours.

Soft-Boiled Eggs (Ajitama)

Boil eggs for exactly 6 minutes 30 seconds, then ice bath. Marinate in broth for 4+ hours for perfect soy-stained yolks.

Nori Placement

Place nori at bowl edge, not submerged. This keeps it crispy and visually appealing for serving.

Green Onion Angles

Cut green onions at a sharp angle for better presentation. White parts for texture, green for garnish.

⚡ Speed Cooking Strategies

Pre-Stage Orders

When you see incoming orders, start boiling water immediately. In multiplayer, assign one player to monitor the kettle constantly.

Multi-Batch Broth

Keep broth at serving temperature in thermal containers. This eliminates waiting time during rush hours.

Topping Prep

Pre-slice chashu at shift start. Have nori, green onions, and naruto pre-portioned. Never prep during service.

Assembly Line

Ladle broth → add noodles → arrange toppings → finish with tare. Always follow the same order for consistency.
