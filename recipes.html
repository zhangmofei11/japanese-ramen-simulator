Recipe Guide

Tonkotsu Ramen (Pork Bone Broth)

The classic creamy white pork broth ramen from Kyushu.

Broth:

  • Pork bones (12+ hours simmer)
  • Pork fat
  • Garlic, ginger, green onion

Toppings:

  • Chashu pork slices
  • Soft-boiled egg (ajitsuke tamago)
  • Green onions, nori
  • Black garlic oil

Shoyu Ramen (Soy Sauce)

Clear brown broth with rich soy sauce flavor.

Broth:

  • Chicken bones
  • Soy sauce base
  • Kombu, dried sardines

Toppings:

  • Chashu pork
  • Bamboo shoots (menma)
  • Nori, green onions

Miso Ramen

Hearty miso-based broth from Hokkaido.

Broth:

  • Chicken and pork bones
  • White or red miso paste
  • Corn, butter

Toppings:

  • Ground pork
  • Corn, bean sprouts
  • Green onions, butter

Shio Ramen (Salt)

Light and clear salt-based broth.

Broth:

  • Chicken bones
  • Sea salt, kombu
  • Light vegetables

Toppings:

  • Ham, chicken slices
  • Spinach, naruto
  • Citrus peel

Cooking Tips

  • Noodle timing - Follow water temperature closely, overcooked noodles ruin the dish
  • Broth consistency - Skim fat regularly for clear broth
  • Topping prep - Prep toppings before service starts
  • Plating - Hold noodles with tongs, don't over-stack toppings
  • Customer patience - Watch the patience meter, serve before it runs out
